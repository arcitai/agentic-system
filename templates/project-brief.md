# Project brief

Revision: 0.2.3 · Updated: 2026-11-25

An optional starting point from Agentic System. Put the useful parts in your
existing project record and adapt its headings to the work. When copying,
replace this template's revision line with your project's own convention.

## Outcome and reader

- What useful result are we working towards, and who will use it?
- What is in scope for this task? What should wait?
- Which model is selected, or who has delegated that choice?

## Sources and context

- Where are the authoritative sources and existing work?
- What does the agent need to understand before acting?
- Which originals must stay in their source system, and what may be retained?

Link to the relevant material rather than copying whole libraries or transcripts.

## Working environment

- Which workspace, tools and service routes are selected for this work?
- What files or data may the agent read or change?
- Which required capability is verified, and what gap affects the next action?

Use the tools the work needs. A repository, Notion home or separate environment
is a project choice. Keep credentials in the selected secret system.

## Authority and delivery

- What actions and delivery has the owner authorized?
- Which consequential choice still needs the responsible person's decision?
- Where should the result and its work record go?

A clear request may already delegate the work and its delivery. Native controls
enforce access; this brief records the intended use and mandate.

## Checks and review

- What would demonstrate a useful, correct result for the intended reader?
- Which sources, tests or other evidence should support it?
- What review does this project require, and which uncertainty remains?

Choose evidence suited to the result: cited reasoning, a checked metric,
a draft reviewed for its audience, a working change or another relevant outcome.

## Continuity and care

- Where can the next person or agent find the current artifact and decisions?
- What must survive an interruption or consequential update?
- Who owns any needed maintenance, recovery or recurring checks?

Keep progress in the project's existing task records. Revisit the brief when
the purpose, sources, authority or operating needs change.
